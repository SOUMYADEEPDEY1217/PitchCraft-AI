# 🎬 PitchCraft AI — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

**Target Duration**: 120 Seconds (2 Minutes)  
**Tone**: Confident, professional, clear, fast-paced  
**Presenter**: Solo Founder / Lead Engineer  
**Resolution**: 1920x1080 (60fps)  

---

### [0:00 – 0:15] The Hook & Problem
- **Visual**: Screen capture of a blank Google Doc titled *"Proposal for Acme Co — v1 Draft"*, cursor blinking uselessly. Then, cut to a messy, 400-word client email brief.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "As a freelance engineer or agency owner, you know the dread: a great client sends an ambiguous project brief, and you're stuck spending four to eight hours drafting a proposal from scratch. Generic AI chatbots hallucinate the math, while traditional SaaS tools charge $50 a month and leak confidential client data. There is a better way."

---

### [0:15 – 0:35] Introducing PitchCraft AI & Brief Analysis
- **Visual**: Cut to PitchCraft AI dashboard. Clean, dark-mode modern interface. The presenter navigates to the **Generator** tab and pastes the unstructured client email.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "Meet PitchCraft AI — the 100% open-source proposal workstation powered by local AI with Ollama and Qwen3. First, I paste our client's raw brief and click **Analyze Brief**."
- **Screen Action**: Click **Analyze Brief**. Within 3 seconds, a modal pops up displaying the 8-dimensional extraction breakdown: Client Objectives, Technical Requirements, Deliverables, Constraints, and Potential Risks.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "Our AI extracts eight crucial dimensions: from core objectives and tech stack constraints to missing discovery questions and hidden execution risks."

---

### [0:35 – 0:55] Proposal Generation with Qwen3
- **Visual**: The presenter clicks **Generate Full Proposal**. A loading progress bar pulses cleanly. After ~8 seconds, the view transitions automatically to the comprehensive Proposal Editor.
- **Screen Action**: Scroll through Tab 1: Executive Summary, Problem Understanding, and Objectives.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "Click **Generate Full Proposal**, and in under ten seconds, Ollama synthesizes an executive-grade document. Every section is generated with precision: strategic context, solution architecture, and scope boundaries."

---

### [0:55 – 1:15] Smart Pricing Calculator & Deterministic Math
- **Visual**: Transition to the **Pricing Calculator** tab.
- **Screen Action**: The presenter enters 80 hours, $125/hr rate, $500 direct software expenses, and a 15% contingency risk buffer. The UI recalculates labor cost ($10,000), subtotal ($10,500), contingency ($1,575), and final quote ($12,075) instantaneously.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "Now watch our pricing engine. Unlike ChatGPT, PitchCraft AI never lets an LLM guess your finances. All calculations are 100% deterministic on our FastAPI backend. We input hours, rate, expenses, and contingency buffer — and our formula calculates the final quote with zero hallucination. One click syncs this directly into the proposal."

---

### [1:15 – 1:35] Advanced Editor, Selective Regeneration & PDF Export
- **Visual**: Back to the **Proposal Editor**. The presenter clicks the **Deliverables & Scope** tab, showing Included vs. Excluded scope.
- **Screen Action**: Click **Regenerate with AI** on the Executive Summary. Enter instruction: *"Make it more urgent and emphasize risk of delay."* The section updates in place without affecting any other tab. Next, switch to **Client Preview**, then click **Download PDF**.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "In the editor, we have full control over deliverables, milestone sprints, and out-of-scope protections. Want to adjust one section? Click **Regenerate**, give it a prompt directive, and only that section is updated. Ready to send? Hit **Download PDF** to generate an executive, ReportLab-rendered document with dynamic tables, terms, and page numbers."
- **Visual**: Quick 3-second display of the crisp, multi-page vector PDF document.

---

### [1:35 – 1:50] Client CRM & AI Business Tools
- **Visual**: Quick cut to the **Client CRM** and **AI Tools** pages.
- **Screen Action**: Show client status change (`Sent` ➔ `Accepted`). Dashboard KPI cards update in real-time. Show the Cover Letter Generator and Follow-Up Email Sequence.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "PitchCraft AI also includes a complete Client CRM to track deals from draft to acceptance, plus AI tools to generate tailored cover letters and follow-up emails based on proposal age."

---

### [1:50 – 2:00] Call-to-Action & Hacktoberfest Wrap-Up
- **Visual**: GitHub repository page and live demo URL on screen with Hacktoberfest 2026 badge.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > "100% private. 100% local. Zero per-token cloud fees. PitchCraft AI is open-source under the MIT license. Check out the live demo and star the repo on GitHub today. Thanks for watching!"
- **Visual**: Screen fades to title card: **PitchCraft AI — Open-Source AI Side Hustle Challenge 2026**.
