Most freelance engineers and agency founders spend 4 to 12 hours writing a single project proposal.

Even worse? 
Generic AI chatbots hallucinate the arithmetic, while traditional proposal SaaS tools charge $50/month and leak confidential client briefs to commercial third-party cloud APIs.

That's why I built **PitchCraft AI** — an open-source, full-stack proposal workstation powered by local AI with Ollama and Qwen3. ⚡

Here is how it changes the game:

1️⃣ **8-Dimensional Brief Extraction**: Paste a messy client email or RFP, and Ollama instantly extracts business objectives, tech requirements, tangible deliverables, missing discovery questions, and hidden scope risks.

2️⃣ **Zero Financial Hallucination**: AI is strictly barred from guessing finances. A deterministic Python engine calculates labor costs, direct expenses, and contingency margins with 100% mathematical accuracy.

3️⃣ **Selective Section Re-prompting**: Made manual edits across 4 tabs and only want to re-phrase the Executive Summary? Click "Regenerate", enter your directive, and update just that section without losing a single manual change elsewhere.

4️⃣ **Executive Vector PDF Export**: Generate multi-page, boardroom-ready PDFs rendered natively via ReportLab with running headers, footers, and page numbers.

5️⃣ **100% Data Sovereignty**: All AI inference runs locally on your machine. Zero cloud per-token fees. Zero NDA violations. Works completely offline.

🛠️ **The Tech Stack**:
• React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4
• FastAPI (Python 3.11+) + Pydantic v2
• SQLAlchemy 2.0 (PostgreSQL / SQLite)
• ReportLab 5.0 PDF Engine
• Ollama + Qwen3:4b LLM
